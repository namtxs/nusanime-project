# Nusanime

Nusanime is an anime streaming platform with a full catalog, a comfortable player, and a clean watch flow — without ads or clutter.

## Platform

Nusanime on the web covers the full streaming experience in the browser: home, search, and episode playback. The interface follows a modern streaming look — dark, content-first, with short navigation.

The catalog includes TV series, exclusive titles, UGC videos, and a daily airing schedule.

## Features

**Home**  
A featured carousel and recommendation rails (trending, new releases, for you, plus genres such as Isekai, Action, Fantasy, and more).

**Index**  
A genre-based catalog for browsing titles systematically.

**Search**  
Find anime or videos from the search bar, with keyword suggestions as you type.

**Schedule**  
See episodes airing each day. Today stays in the center; swipe dates to switch the episode list.

**Trending & Premium**  
Ranked titles that are popular right now, plus an exclusive/premium catalog.

**Player**  
Play episodes in DASH quality, and pick server and resolution. For long-running series like One Piece, episode sections slide sideways and the episode list scrolls, matching the player height.

**History & My List**  
History keeps one row per anime: the last episode and the last timestamp. My List saves titles to watch later. Quality and server preferences are stored in the browser.

## Why Nusanime

- **Built for watching** — a clean interface, no noise, no third-party accounts
- **Accurate continue watching** — history is not duplicated per episode; it always shows the latest episode and progress
- **A full home page** — sections stay populated; genres fill in when featured data is thin
- **A clear schedule** — Aired / Upcoming status, with easy day navigation
- **A complete player** — quality, server, and the episode list on one screen
- **Fast in the browser** — a modern web app, no separate install
